How a commission runs

  1. Brief & references. You send goals, references and a deadline; I confirm scope and price.
  2. Blockout approval. A rough sculpt/model is shared for shape and proportion sign-off.
  3. Full build. Sculpting or modeling, texturing and lighting to final quality.
  4. Delivery. Final renders (and source files, if licensed) delivered on the agreed date.

Good fits for a commission:

  • Original characters and creatures
  • Stylized props and hero assets
  • Environment or set-dressing pieces
  • Turntable/render packages for portfolios or store pages
